01 / GET TO KNOW IT

What is a
peony?

A peony is a flowering plant known for its lush, layered petals and wonderfully full blooms. It comes in shades of blush, ivory, coral, red, and deep pink — each one opening like a little cloud of color.

Peonies are loved in gardens and bouquets for their romantic look, gentle fragrance, and timeless elegance. Their blooming season is brief, which makes every blossom feel extra special.
